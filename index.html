How it works

On court in three steps

  1. 01

    Pick your sport & time

    Choose padel, football or table tennis and the slot that suits your group. We open at 4 PM Sunday to Wednesday, and 3 PM Thursday to Saturday.

  2. 02

    Book it

    Reserve on Playtomic for instant confirmation, or send us the details on WhatsApp and we'll arrange it. You can always just call.

  3. 03

    Show up & play

    We confirm your slot, park you close to the court, and hand over the gear. You bring the shoes.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to bring my own racket?

No. Rackets and balls are available at reception for both padel and table tennis. Bring clean court shoes and you're set.

Can I come without a full group?

Yes — message us and we'll try to match you with players of a similar level, or point you to an open pick-up game.

How far ahead should I book?

Weeknight prime time (7–11 PM) fills quickly — a day or two ahead is safest. Earlier slots are usually available same-day.

Is there parking?

Yes, free on-site parking right next to the courts.

Do you host tournaments or corporate events?

We do. Call or WhatsApp us with your group size and preferred date and we'll put a package together.

The court's free tonight.

Round up four people and we'll see you at 7.
